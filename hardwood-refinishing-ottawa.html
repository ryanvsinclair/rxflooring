FAQ

Sanding & Refinishing questions.

How much does hardwood refinishing cost in Ottawa?

Square footage, stairs, stain vs clear, and how deep the wear goes all matter. We assess on site and give a fixed price in writing. The assessment is free.

How dusty is the process?

We use dust-controlled sanding and contain the work area. Fine dust still happens. We tell you what to expect and what to cover before we start.

Can you refinish stairs too?

Yes. Stairs are quoted separately because they take more time and detail than a flat field.

How long until we can walk on it?

Usually within a day for light traffic, longer before rugs and heavy furniture. We leave a clear schedule for your finish system.

How soon can you come out?

Usually within a few days. Assessments run Monday to Saturday, 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Book online or call (613) 890-3394.
