FAQ

Flooring Removal questions.

How much does flooring removal cost in Ottawa?

Depends on the product, square footage, stairs, and how it is glued or fastened. We assess on site and give a fixed price in writing.

Do you haul everything away?

Yes. Tear-out includes haul-away unless you ask to keep materials.

Can you remove glue-down floors?

Yes. Glue-down takes longer and is priced accordingly.

Will you protect the rest of the house?

Yes. Paths and adjacent finishes are protected during tear-out.

How soon can you come out?

Usually within a few days. Assessments run Monday to Saturday, 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Book online or call (613) 890-3394.
