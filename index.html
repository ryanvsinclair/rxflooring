The prescription

The same four steps, every job.

Most flooring quotes are a number on a page. Every RX job starts with a diagnosis and ends with a document you can hold the work against.

01 — DIAGNOSE

On site, not over the phone

Room-by-room measure, subfloor inspection, moisture and level readings. Ray identifies what is actually wrong before anyone talks about product.

02 — PRESCRIBE

A written spec

Material and underlay, plank direction, seam plan, transitions, prep required, start date and a fixed price. One page. No line reading "misc."

03 — INSTALL

The crew that measured it

Furniture moved, old flooring hauled away, dust contained, site swept daily. Ray is on the job, not dispatching it from a truck across town.

04 — SIGN OFF

Walkthrough and warranty

Deficiency walk with you before final payment, then written workmanship warranty. The same close-out process used on 750 builder units last year.

Common diagnoses

Tell us the symptom.

You do not need to know the name of the problem. These are three of the most common calls in Ottawa homes, and what each one actually takes.

“There's a ridge running down the hallway.”

Diagnosis

Carpet has relaxed off the tack strip — normal after three to five years, worse over radiant heat.

Treatment

Power-stretch and re-tack. No new carpet required.

Duration

Half a day

“You can see the seam in the living room.”

Diagnosis

Seam cut off-pattern and closed without a sealer, so the edges have opened and frayed.

Treatment

Re-cut on pattern, hot-melt tape with edge sealer, roll and weight overnight.

Duration

One day

“The planks are lifting near the patio door.”

Diagnosis

Slab moisture, or no expansion gap left at the perimeter — the floor has nowhere to move.

Treatment

Moisture test, vapour barrier where the reading calls for it, re-lay with correct gap and transitions.

Duration

Two to three days

    The founder

    Ray

    Photo to come Chest-up, plain background, natural light. A phone photo is fine.

    Ray has been installing flooring professionally for close to ten years, and the trade caught him from day one.

    There's a real craft to it — seaming carpet so the joins disappear, wrapping a staircase cleanly around every spindle, lining up a patterned carpet so the whole room reads as one continuous piece. Getting that exactly right became the standard he holds every job to.

    After moving to Canada almost seven years ago, Ray built on that foundation. For the past three years he has worked as a trusted subcontractor and project manager for one of Ottawa's flooring companies, handling measuring, scheduling, installation and warranty. In 2025 alone he was responsible for more than 750 units across major Ottawa-area developments — including sites built by Minto, Mattamy, Caivan and Claridge — coordinating more than ten installation crews at once and seeing each home through from first measurement to final walkthrough with the homeowner.

    That experience shaped the way Ray runs a job today: on schedule, on budget, done right the first time. It is also why this business exists — to bring that same craftsmanship and reliability directly to homeowners, across flooring and the full range of renovation services.
