Prep decides whether tile lasts

Lippage, hollow spots, and cracked grout usually start under the tile. We flatten the substrate, choose the right thinset and membrane, and set to a consistent joint. Showers, entries, and heated floors get the detail they need, not a one-size layout.

FAQ

Tile Installation questions.

How much does tile installation cost in Ottawa?

Tile size, pattern, substrate condition, waterproofing, and tear-out drive the number. We measure on site and give a fixed price in writing. Assessment is free.

Do you do showers and bathrooms?

Yes. Wet areas get the membrane and slope detail the room requires, not just tile stuck to drywall.

Large-format tile: can you install it?

Yes, with the right prep and thinset. Large format fails fast on an uneven floor, which is why we assess first.

How long does tile installation take?

A typical bathroom floor is one to two days plus cure time before heavy use. Full renovations take longer.

How soon can you come out?

Usually within a few days. Assessments run Monday to Saturday, 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Book online or call (613) 890-3394.
