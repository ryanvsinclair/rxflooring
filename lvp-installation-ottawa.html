Click-lock or glue-down vinyl

LVP and LVT fail when the floor underneath is ignored. We check moisture, flatten highs and lows, and choose click-lock or glue-down based on the room, not whatever is easiest to sell. Transitions, underlayment, and expansion gaps are part of the install, not extras you discover on invoice day.

We cover Ottawa and surrounding communities, move furniture when needed, haul the old floor, and leave a fixed number in writing before product is ordered.

FAQ

LVP / LVT Installation questions.

How much does LVP installation cost in Ottawa?

It depends on square footage, the product, subfloor condition, and whether old flooring has to come out. We measure on site and give you a fixed price in writing. The assessment is free.

Is LVP good for basements?

Yes. Luxury vinyl handles moisture better than laminate or hardwood. We still check the slab and use the right underlayment or glue-down method for the space.

Do you remove the old floor?

Yes. Tear-out and haul-away are quoted up front so there are no surprises.

How long does LVP installation take?

Most single rooms are one day. Open-concept main floors are typically one to two days once prep is done.

How soon can you come out?

Usually within a few days. Assessments run Monday to Saturday, 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Book online or call (613) 890-3394.
