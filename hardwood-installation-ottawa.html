The method matches the floor

Hardwood is unforgiving when moisture and fastening are guessed. We acclimate the boards, verify the subfloor, and pick nail-down, glue-down, or floating based on the product and the structure — not habit. Stairs, vents, and transitions are planned before the first board goes down.

Serving Ottawa and nearby communities. Tear-out quoted up front, furniture handled, and a written fixed price before materials are ordered.

01

Site and moisture

FAQ

Hardwood Installation questions.

How much does hardwood installation cost in Ottawa?

Species, width, solid vs engineered, square footage, and prep all matter. We measure on site and give a fixed price in writing. The assessment is free.

Solid or engineered — which should I get?

Depends on the subfloor, below-grade spaces, and the look you want. We recommend based on the room, not a one-size pitch.

Do you sand and refinish too?

Yes — new installs and existing hardwood. Ask for sanding and refinishing when you book the assessment.

How long does hardwood installation take?

Most rooms are one to three days depending on size and pattern. Finishing schedules add time if we are staining on site.

How soon can you come out?

Usually within a few days. Assessments run Monday to Saturday, 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Book online or call (613) 890-3394.
